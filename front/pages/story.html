Meet our bridal party

Gabriel Wong

Best Man

Growing up, Gabe and Simeon spent countless hours building with LEGO, tearing apart electronics, and playing with stuffed animals. Although Gabe is two years his junior, Simeon finds incredible inspiration in Gabe's faith, character, and talent. Gabriel has moved to Hong Kong for work, and they've continued to bond through travels to Japan, diving lessons in the Philippines, and video calls when their schedules align.

Iris Hsueh Ng

Matron of Honour

Together, Iris and Jess have traveled to:Greece, Italy, New York, Chicago, and Ottawa. Iris nearly died practicing driving with Jess, and Jess nearly drowned trying to teach Iris to swim.
In the future they hope to avoid near death experiences, and go to Disneyland together!

Terry Pyun

Groomsman

Terry and Simeon met at New Hope Fellowship, then remet at New Hope Fellowship a few months later. They nerd out over technology, board games, and working together on the praise and audio-visual teams for church service.

Jasmine Yeung

Bridesmaid

There is no one with whom Jess has laughed harder and longer than with Jasmine. Their friendship dates back to a time when convenience store popsicles were 50¢ a pop. For just 25¢ each, they would share a sweet after school popsicle and, unwittingly grow a sweeter friendship. They remain, hardcore snackers and steadfast friends.

Raven Landicho

Groomsman

Raven and Simeon bonded over a shared love for dark memes, board games, and coming up with the cheesiest pickup lines. When Simeon bandwagons onto our winning teams, Raven graciously explains what's happening. Raven also climbs with Simeon exactly one (1) time per year.

Carol Tse Fang

Bridesmaid

Carol has shaped Jess’ life as a leader, mentor, and friend. Although separated by the Atlantic Ocean for several years, the two kept in touch with snail mail and email. Legend has it, Carol still has several strands of hair that Jess mailed her (there was one in every letter). Carol is one of those rare friends that has always helped shape Jess' identity for the better, throughout every stage of her life.

Rochelle Chong

Groomswoman

Rochelle and Simeon both think out loud, naturally annoying most other friends except each other. They've been friends since childhood, and were actually flower girl and ring bearer together many years ago. Simeon's jokes about putting recycling in the trash continue to be a constant source of consternation.

Andrew Leung

Bridesman

Andy and Jess became fast friends in Sunday school in the Spring of 2006. At the time, Andy was shorter than Jess, and bald. Much like the development of his height and facial hair, their friendship has bloomed as they learned to support one another through family losses, breakups, and volunteering in Church together.

Henry Laurence Ho

Ring Bearer

Henry is Jess' favourite nephew and possibly the first baby she has held for over 2 minutes. It is Jess' firm belief that Henry is the smartest baby around. Jess and Simeon are thrilled to have Henry in their bridal party.

Trinity Luu

Flower Girl

Trinity has held the position as Jess' wedding dance partner since 2018. Together this dynamic duo have torn up rugs and built blanket forts. This wedding would not be complete without Jess' special dance partner!
